Welcome to UC Library Search

UC Library Search connects you with the library collections at all 10 University of California campuses, plus additional databases to help you locate millions of resources.

Use UC Library Search to locate scholarly and peer-reviewed articles, books, ebooks, audiovisual materials, digital media, tech lending items, primary resources and special collections, dissertations, government documents, course reserves, maps and more!

Search Tips
  1. To limit your search to books and other materials held by UC San Diego Library (except articles), select the Library Catalog search option that appears when you begin typing.
  2. Use AND and OR (uppercase) to combine search concepts:
    • youth AND voting
    • self-driving OR "autonomous vehicles"
  3. Use wildcard operators * and ? to search for word variations:
    • ocean*= ocean, oceans, oceanic, oceanography, etc.
    • colo?r = color, colour
  4. Use quotation marks to search exact phrases:
    • "social media"
