These examples are intended to reflect typical student behaviors: informal language, direct requests for help, mentions of class requirements, and a focus on "finding" rather than deeply understanding the research at first.

Use each example as a three-turn test script:

1. **Realistic Student Prompt** — what the student might first type.
2. **Intake Context** — the additional details the student provides after the skill asks clarifying questions.
3. **Narrowed Topic Context** — the likely follow-up after the landscape response, when the student chooses themes or questions to explore further.

## Common Undergraduate Research Assignments & Realistic Student Prompts

### 1. **Annotated Bibliography (Social Sciences)**
**Assignment:** Research assignment in preparation for Psychology capstone. Find and summarize 8-10 scholarly sources on the student's chosen topic: impact of social media on college student mental health.

**Realistic Student Prompt:** 
"i need to find sources about social media and mental health for college students. can you help me find some good articles? need like 8-10 for my psychology class"

**Intake Context:** 
- "It's for my Psychology 190 capstone prep assignment."
- "I need 8-10 scholarly sources, preferably peer-reviewed, in APA style."
- "Focus on college students around age 18-24. I'm interested in anxiety, depression, sleep, and social comparison, but I don't want news articles."
- "Recent sources would be better — maybe 2018 to now."

**Narrowed Topic Context:** 
- "The social comparison theme looks most useful. Can you make a reading list on how passive scrolling and social comparison affect anxiety or self-esteem?"
- "I'd also like a few sources on sleep disruption, but keep the focus on college students."

### 2. **Literature Review (Business)**
**Assignment:** Write a 10-page literature review examining leadership styles in modern organizations.

**Realistic Student Prompt:**
"what are the main leadership styles that researchers talk about? I have to write a lit review on leadership in business and need to know what studies have found"

**Intake Context:** 
- "It's a 10-page literature review for my Business 301 class."
- "I need about 12-15 peer-reviewed sources, mostly from 2015 to 2025."
- "We're supposed to compare leadership styles and discuss what outcomes they affect."
- "I'd like to include transformational, transactional, servant, and maybe one other style."

**Narrowed Topic Context:** 
- "Transformational and servant leadership look most relevant. Can you focus on employee engagement and turnover?"
- "I'd also like a couple of sources on remote or hybrid teams if that shows up in the research."

---

### 3. **Comparative Analysis Paper (History)**
**Assignment:** Compare and contrast the causes of the French Revolution and the American Revolution using primary and secondary sources.

**Realistic Student Prompt:**
"can you explain the main causes of the french revolution vs american revolution? I need to write a paper comparing them and need help understanding what historians say about why they happened"

**Intake Context:** 
- "It's a comparative analysis paper for History 210."
- "I need 8-10 sources, including primary sources and modern historical scholarship."
- "We're supposed to compare causes, not just narrate events."
- "I'd prefer recent scholarly interpretations plus a few primary sources from the 1700s."

**Narrowed Topic Context:** 
- "The taxation and representation theme looks strongest. Can you focus on how fiscal crisis and political representation differed?"
- "I'd also like a source or two on Enlightenment ideas, but the main comparison should be political and economic causes."

---

### 4. **Case Study Analysis (Nursing/Healthcare)**
**Assignment:** Analyze a healthcare policy implementation using evidence-based research on patient outcomes.

**Realistic Student Prompt:**
"I'm doing a case study on the affordable care act and need research about how it affected patient outcomes. what are the main findings from studies?"

**Intake Context:** 
- "It's a case study for my health policy class."
- "I need 8-10 scholarly sources, preferably peer-reviewed and from 2010 to 2024."
- "My instructor wants me to connect policy implementation to patient outcomes."
- "I'd like to focus on insurance coverage, preventive care, chronic disease management, and disparities."

**Narrowed Topic Context:** 
- "Medicaid expansion looks like the clearest theme. Can you make a reading list on how expansion affected coverage and access to care?"
- "I'd also like one or two sources on chronic disease outcomes, especially diabetes or hypertension."

### 5. **Scientific Research Proposal (Biology)**
**Assignment:** Develop a research proposal investigating factors affecting plant growth, including literature review and methodology.

**Realistic Student Prompt:**
"what factors affect plant growth? need to write a proposal for my bio lab and have to cite actual studies about things like light, water, soil ph etc"

**Intake Context:** 
- "It's a research proposal for Biology 101L."
- "I need 6-10 empirical studies, not just general background."
- "We're supposed to include a short literature review and a methods section."
- "I'm thinking about a controlled greenhouse experiment, maybe using tomato seedlings."

**Narrowed Topic Context:** 
- "Soil pH and nutrient availability look most useful. Can you focus on how those affect seedling growth?"
- "I'd also like one or two studies that compare light intensity, but pH should be the main factor."

### 6. **Argumentative Research Paper (English Composition)**
**Assignment:** Write an argumentative essay on renewable energy adoption, supported by at least 6 credible sources.

**Realistic Student Prompt:**
"help me find arguments for why we should use more renewable energy. need sources for a research paper, like what are the benefits and why should governments invest in it"

**Intake Context:** 
- "It's an argumentative research paper for English 102."
- "I need at least 6 credible sources, preferably from 2015 to 2025."
- "The paper should take a clear position and address counterarguments."
- "I can use scholarly sources and policy reports, but not random blogs."

**Narrowed Topic Context:** 
- "The economic benefits and grid reliability themes look strongest. Can you focus on job creation, long-term costs, and energy stability?"
- "I'd also like one source that presents a counterargument about cost or implementation challenges so I can respond to it."

### 7. **Cultural Analysis Essay (Anthropology/Sociology)**
**Assignment:** Research and analyze the role of festivals in community identity formation.

**Realistic Student Prompt:**
"how do festivals affect community identity? im writing about cultural traditions and need research on why festivals matter to communities"

**Intake Context:** 
- "It's a cultural analysis essay for Anthropology 102."
- "I need 5-7 scholarly sources, preferably from anthropology or sociology."
- "I'm interested in how festivals create belonging and pass on traditions."
- "I'd prefer sources from 2000 onward, and I don't want sources that only talk about tourism."

**Narrowed Topic Context:** 
- "The ritual and collective belonging theme looks most useful. Can you focus on how festivals reinforce shared identity?"
- "I'd also like a source or two on immigrant or diaspora festivals if that appears in the results."

- "I need 8-10 scholarly sources, mostly from 2010 onward."

**Narrowed Topic Context:**

### 10. **Educational Policy Research Paper (Education)**
**Assignment:** Examine the effectiveness of different teaching methods in elementary education based on peer-reviewed research.

**Realistic Student Prompt:**
"what does research say about different teaching methods for elementary school? like which ones work best? need sources for my education paper"

**Intake Context:** 
- "It's a 10-page policy research paper for Education 210."
- "I need 10-12 peer-reviewed sources, mostly from 2010 onward."
- "The paper should focus on elementary school, roughly kindergarten through fifth grade."
- "I'd like to compare direct instruction, project-based learning, and differentiated instruction."

**Narrowed Topic Context:** 
- "Small-group differentiated instruction and project-based learning look strongest. Can you focus on early literacy and math outcomes?"
- "I'd also like a source or two about implementation challenges for teachers."
