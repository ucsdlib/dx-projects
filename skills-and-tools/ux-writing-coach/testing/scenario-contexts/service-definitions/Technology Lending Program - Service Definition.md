# Technology Lending Program \- Service Definition

## Description

The Tech Lending Program (TLP) circulates technology items to current UC San Diego faculty, staff, and students to ensure that users have the technological means to conduct research and complete coursework as efficiently and effectively as possible.

## Service Vision

Articulate the forward-facing vision for this service - where are we now (what is the purpose of the service) and where are we headed next? 

Meet the evolving technology needs of our users to support their success inside and outside the classroom and close the technology equity gap.

## Service Scope

TLP inventory consists mostly of frequently-requested items like device chargers, adapters (e.g., USB-C Hub to HDMI), portable DVD players, and headphones.

| **In Scope ** | **Out of Scope** |
| --- | --- |
| High demand, universal cables/dongles to power common computing devices (charging cables) | Niche dongles/connectors for outdated equipment |
| Testing support equipment (calculators) | Devices that require software updates or that pose a security risk due to potential for shared information (computers) |
| Equipment/items to facilitate access to Library Collections (headphones/Blu-ray players, remotes, tote bags) | (replacements for personal power cords) |
|  | Consumables, including items with limited life span |

## Decision Making

Selection of items to be included in TLP as well as removal is based on user and staff feedback as well as circulation statistics.  The Program manager, in consultation with the UX APD with make data informed decisions based on the evolving needs of our users and their technology in line with the service description and vision.
