# Ask Service Definition

## Description

Add a short description of the service

This front-line service provides quick answers, orientation, and referrals for anyone contacting the UC San Diego Library. Interactions are designed to be brief—typically under five minutes—and focus on resolving common questions, clarifying policies and spaces, or connecting users to the right expertise or resource.

Support is available in person at Library service points, online via email and web forms, and by phone. When needs are more in-depth, staff route users to the appropriate Library, campus, or community service for specialized assistance or follow-up.

## Service Vision

Articulate the forward-facing vision for this service - where are we now (what is the purpose of the service) and where are we headed next? 

Provide a welcoming, reliable first point of contact for all Library users—delivering quick answers, essential orientation, and timely referrals that connect people to the spaces, services, and resources best suited to meet their needs.

## Service Scope

This service is intended to be primarily a quick answer and referral point.  As such, most interactions are expected to be under 5 minutes in length. Should a user need more one on one assistance they may be referred to another library, campus, or community service.

### In Scope

- Assisting users with known item searches, including introduction to UC Library Search
- Informing users regarding available Library spaces, services, and amenities as well as Library policies
- Providing referral or directions to other campus services and locations
- Assistance with library equipment, including campus print service

### Out of Scope

- Research question or topic development
- Personalized research support or training
- Assistance with personal technology beyond that related to eligible Library services

## Service Calendar

There are a number of important dates throughout the year that see an increase in questions, or support for a designated group.  The list below is by no means exhaustive, but highlights some of the known events and their audiences.  Primary services as well as tabling or outreach activities may be offered.

- Summer: Undergrad and Grad student orientations
- Welcome Week/Week 0 of Fall Term: All students and faculty, new and continuing
- Homecoming Week/end (Fall): Alumni and donors
- Triton Day (2nd Saturday of April): Transfer and prospective students
- Spring Commencement (2nd weekend in June): Graduates and their families

The User Experience Program also aims to host regular Public Services Gatherings with the goal of bringing service providers from across the library together and facilitating communication among groups.  These gatherings are usually held during intersessions and/or summer when service demands are lower.
