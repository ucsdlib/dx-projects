### Turn 3 — Skill

> **What works**
> - Task-first titles are parallel and scannable.
> - Card 2's "ask us" and "we'll retrieve" feel warm and personal.
> - Card 3's plain language is a good call over "non-circulating."
>
> **Top opportunities**
>
> 1. **Card 1 doesn't say who can borrow.** A visitor might assume they can check out materials, then discover they can't.
> 2. **Card 2's "non-public or offsite locations" is opaque.** A first-time visitor may not know what these areas are or why materials are there.
> 3. **No card explicitly welcomes visitors.** Your eligibility facts say onsite collections are open to all, but the cards don't say so.
>
> Hypothesis: first-time visitors may not realize that borrowing and using materials in the Library have different eligibility rules. User testing would confirm.
>
> **Suggested revisions**
>
> **Borrow eligible materials**
> - Summary: Check out eligible items to use outside the Library. Available to UC San Diego affiliates and registered community borrowers.
> - CTA: See borrowing options
>
> **Request items for pickup**
> - Summary: Some materials are kept in storage or non-public areas. Request them and we'll retrieve them for pickup.
> - CTA: Request an item
>
> **Use materials in the Library**
> - Summary: Use materials in the Library, including items that can't be borrowed. Open to all visitors.
> - CTA: See on-site use details
>
> **Why**
>
> - Card 1 adds eligibility after the action, preserving the "open door" feel ([Library UX](https://ucsdlibrary.atlassian.net/wiki/x/AoC_0)).
> - Card 2 replaces "non-public or offsite locations" with "storage or non-public areas," which is more concrete.
> - Card 3 now says "open to all visitors," making the inclusive pathway explicit and distinguishing it from card 1's restricted borrowing.
> - "Some items are Library use only" is covered across cards 1 and 3 without repeating it in either summary.
>
> **Next steps**
>
> - Verify "UC San Diego affiliates" is the public-facing term, or whether "students, faculty, and staff" is clearer.
> - Confirm whether requesting items for pickup is open to all visitors or has its own eligibility limits.
> - User-test card 2's wording with first-time visitors.
>
> Tell me which revision to refine or what constraint to add.
