### Turn 3 — Skill

> **Recommendation**
> - Keep the under-five-minute expectation if the service team can support it as a public statement. Use “Most questions take less than five minutes,” not “usually under five minutes.” If it’s an internal performance target rather than a user-facing commitment, remove it until confirmed.
> - Show the out-of-scope items, but frame them as a public boundary and pair them with the referral path. This helps users self-route without exposing internal workflow language. [Library UX](https://ucsdlibrary.atlassian.net/wiki/x/AoC_0)
>
> **Suggested copy**
> Most questions take less than five minutes. When you need more in-depth help, staff will connect you with the appropriate Library, campus, or community service.
>
> ## When we’ll refer you
> Ask is for brief help. We don’t provide:
> - Help developing a research question or topic
> - Personalized research support or training
> - Help with personal technology beyond eligible Library services
>
