# Scenario A5: Ask Contact and Support Path

## Focus

Test intake and drafting for a contact and support molecule.

## Exact User Turns

### Turn 1

```text
Our Ask page has a generic “Contact Us” tile, but most people need one of three things: directions or a known-item search, help with Library equipment or campus printing, or a referral for an in-depth research question. Please help me improve the help section.

Available pathways are the Geisel Library Front Desk, WongAvery Front Desk, Library main telephone line, Contact Us form, and in-depth research consultations.
```

### Turn 2

```text
Use a task-specific contact and support structure. Lead with when to use each pathway and give the pathway name, but do not include hours or links because I have not verified them.
```

### Turn 3

```text
Would a single “Ask the Library” heading work better than three separate task headings?
```

## Success Criteria

- Shows a compact plan-only turn with `Goal`, `Audience`, `Top jobs`, `Approach`, and `To confirm`.
- Ends the plan turn with the review invitation and does not include the full contact and support section.
- Drafts the task-specific pathways in Turn 2 after the user confirms the structure.
- Builds task-specific pathways rather than one generic contact tile.
- Uses the contact and support molecule.
- Distinguishes quick help from research support.
- Does not fabricate hours, URLs, response times, or staff names.
- Notes unverified links and hours.
- Responds to the structure question with a reasoned recommendation.
