# Search strategies — extreme heat and urban health/wellbeing

## Working question

How does extreme heat affect health and wellbeing in urban communities?

## UC Library Search — focused peer-reviewed strategy

**Copy-paste query**

```
("extreme heat" OR "heat waves" OR heatwave*) AND (urban OR city OR cities) AND (mortality OR morbidity OR hospitalization OR "emergency department" OR "mental health" OR sleep OR wellbeing OR "well-being" OR vulnerability)
```

**[Run in UC Library Search](https://search-library.ucsd.edu/discovery/search?query=any,contains,(%22extreme%20heat%22%20OR%20%22heat%20waves%22%20OR%20heatwave*)%20AND%20(urban%20OR%20city%20OR%20cities)%20AND%20(mortality%20OR%20morbidity%20OR%20hospitalization%20OR%20%22emergency%20department%22%20OR%20%22mental%20health%22%20OR%20sleep%20OR%20wellbeing%20OR%20%22well-being%22%20OR%20vulnerability)&tab=ArticleBooksEtc&search_scope=ArticlesBooksEtc&vid=01UCS_SDI:UCSD&mfacet=tlevel,include,peer_reviewed,1&mfacet=rtype,include,articles,1&facet=searchcreationdate,include,2020%7C,%7C2026,lk)**

Filters: Peer-reviewed journals, Articles, 2020–2026.

## Next refinement directions

- Replace outcome terms with a population lens: homelessness, older adults, children, outdoor workers.
- Replace outcome terms with a context lens: green space, housing policy, urban heat island, blackouts.
- Use title-field search for a higher-precision set when result volume is overwhelming.
- Search PubMed for clinical/epidemiological studies and Scopus/Web of Science for citation chaining.

## Domain and citation-chaining databases

- **PubMed** — useful for mortality, morbidity, mental-health, emergency-care, and older-adult studies.
- **Web of Science or Scopus** — useful for cited-reference chaining around strong review or landmark papers.
- **GreenFILE or Environment Index** — useful for green infrastructure, urban form, and adaptation-policy literature.
