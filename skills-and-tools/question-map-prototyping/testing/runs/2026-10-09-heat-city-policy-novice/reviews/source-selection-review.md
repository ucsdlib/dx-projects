## Coverage summary

- **Source precision:** 20/20 selected records are topically relevant and none is irrelevant or unsupported. However, the selected set is broad: all 20 independent pool entries were retained, with no evidence of adaptive pruning against the novice city-stakeholder use case.
- **Reference recall:** The pool and selected set cover all six required components. Health outcomes, equity, governance, and lived experience each have at least one strong anchor; urban form and measurement limitations are covered but less deeply operationalized.

- **Missing disciplines, methods, populations, or geographies:** Not found in the selected records are cost-effectiveness or economic evaluation, contemporary U.S. municipal implementation studies, hospital/ambulance/primary-care use, disability-specific studies, renters or low-income housing, outdoor workers, recent participatory lived-experience research, and low- and middle-income-country evidence.
- **Overrepresented literatures or source types:** Systematic reviews and quantitative U.S./European studies are prominent, with three governance-focused reviews (S12, S14, S15) and multiple national health-outcome studies. Metadata-only closed-access records are common enough to constrain later claim extraction.
