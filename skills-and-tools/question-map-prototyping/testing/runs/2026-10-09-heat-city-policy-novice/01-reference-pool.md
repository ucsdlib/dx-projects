## Coverage summary

- **Source precision:** 19/20 entries are scholarly publications; the remaining entry is operational international guidance. All entries are directly heat- or temperature-health-focused.
- **Reference recall:** The pool spans all six required components, with health outcomes, equity, urban form, governance, lived experience, and measurement limitations each supported by at least two entries.
- **Component coverage:** Strongest for health outcomes and governance. Equity is supported by both index-based and access/adaptive-capacity studies. Urban cooling is supported by systematic review, city-level assessment, and health-impact modeling.
- **Missing disciplines, methods, populations, or geographies:** The pool is U.S./Europe/Australia weighted and lacks sufficient low- and middle-income-country evidence, disability-specific studies, economic evaluations, and recent lived-experience research.
- **Overrepresented literatures or source types:** Systematic reviews are prominent, and quantitative U.S. health studies outnumber operational evaluations. Gray literature is limited to one international guidance document.
- **Evidence caution:** Local transfer should not assume identical vulnerability profiles, health-system effects, urban morphology, housing conditions, or governance capacity.
