# Search Strategies

**Run:** `2026-10-09-heat-city-policy-novice-v2`  
**Working question:** What should my city do about extreme heat and public health? I want recent research evidence to bring to a city council meeting.  
**Breadth:** Comprehensive.  
**Date scope:** 2016–2026, with the exception of one older empirical evaluation (`10.1155/2018/7973519`) retained for its direct evidence about heat action plan effects.  
**Execution:** UC Library Search first through the keyless Primo PNX REST fallback. OpenAlex and Crossref were used for DOI verification and citation metadata. PDF extraction was not used.

## Verification and selection

Each selected article was matched by DOI to OpenAlex and Crossref. Citation counts from both providers were retained separately. The evidence base is primarily abstract plus metadata; no PDF text was extracted. `src_015` has metadata-only evidence because OpenAlex, Crossref, and Semantic Scholar did not return an abstract; its source question is low confidence.
