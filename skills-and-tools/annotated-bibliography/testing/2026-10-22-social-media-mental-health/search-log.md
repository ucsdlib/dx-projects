# Search log

## Scope

**Working question:** How does social media affect mental health among college students, with attention to anxiety, depression, sleep, and social comparison?

**Breadth:** focused landscape followed by selected comprehensive deep work.

**Execution mode:** UC Library Search first; keyless Primo PNX REST fallback; no keyed Primo Search API.

## Query summary

### `landscape`

- **Tool:** UC Library Search / Primo PNX REST fallback
- **Purpose:** Focused landscape across social media, mental health, and college students
- **Query:** `((((("social media") OR ("social networking sites"))) AND ((("mental health") OR (anxiety) OR (depression)))) AND ((("college students") OR ("undergraduate students"))))`
- **Results:** 2,727 reported; 20 returned; 20 unique records screened.
- **Approximate fidelity:** 75%.
- **Decision:** Selected 7 records and ran two component searches because the first page retained relevant but conceptually separable branches.

## Verification

Crossref metadata was retrieved for all 12 selected sources. All source IDs had matching titles and verified container/year metadata. No metadata conflicts were identified.

## Selection decisions

- `landscape-03` — selected: Systematic review of SNS-based youth mental-health interventions; directly maps an intervention branch.
- `landscape-05` — selected: Review linking problematic SNS use to depression, anxiety, stress, sleep, and emerging brain-function evidence.
- `landscape-10` — selected: Survey and interview study of peer recognition, stigma, and support on SNSs; represents a social-context branch.
- `landscape-11` — selected: Cross-sectional student study linking SNS use, impression management, social comparison, depression, and anxiety.
- `landscape-14` — selected: Three-wave college-freshman panel study testing compensatory SNS use, family support, and depression.
- `landscape-15` — selected: Randomized experiment testing whether limiting social media improves loneliness, depression, anxiety, and FOMO.
- `landscape-19` — selected: Systematic review of envy, social comparison, and depression, including evidence on direction and moderators.
- `component-03` — selected: Three-college-student study set testing self-esteem and FOMO as mediators between SNS use and depression.
- `component-10` — selected: Systematic review organizing SNS-use effects on self-esteem through comparison, feedback, and self-reflection.
- `component-18` — selected: Meta-analysis testing whether SNS-use indicators predict well-being after accounting for social support and self-esteem.
- `sleep-02` — selected: Foundational undergraduate study of social-media timing and sleep quality; retained as a 2016 exception to the preferred date range.
- `sleep-04` — selected: Cross-sectional study linking problematic SNS use with sleep quality and mental-health symptoms.

## Limitations

- No keyed Primo Search API was available; the keyless PNX REST fallback was used.
- All annotations are abstract-level; no full text was reviewed.
- The 2016 sleep source is a deliberate exception to the preferred 2018-2026 range because it directly studies undergraduate sleep timing.
- The Primo PNX response includes primarily local and Primo Central metadata; result counts should not be treated as complete field coverage.
- The landscape is not exhaustive and is best understood as purpose-aware saturation for the undergraduate assignment.
- The test simulated intake and narrowed-topic context from assignment-prompts.md rather than a live multi-turn conversation.
