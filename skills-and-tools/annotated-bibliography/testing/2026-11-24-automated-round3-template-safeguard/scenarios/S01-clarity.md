# Scenario S01: Template-safeguard rerun (round 3)

Adapted from `2026-11-24-automated-round2-clarity/scenarios/S01-clarity.md`. User turns are verbatim identical to rounds 1-2; expected behavior adds the template-defaulting safeguard and rule 7 on top of the round 2 plain-language contract.

## Focus

**Mode or workflow:** Intake, landscape (as "overview of the research"), selection, refinement (narrowing), adaptive follow-up.

**Assumed role:** A senior psychology undergraduate preparing a capstone literature review. All clarity judgments are made from this persona's perspective.

**Activation expectation:** Explicit invocation line at Turn 1 (routing deferred, per round-1 decision).

## Exact User Turns

Identical to rounds 1-2 S01 (see the round 1 card for the verbatim turns).

## Expected Behavior and Success Criteria

Rounds 1-2 behaviors must still hold: focused landscape default, proportionate narrowing with source reuse, honest evidence labeling, adaptive turn-3 follow-up, manifest validation, and the full plain-language contract.

Template safeguard (new in this round):

- The landscape response keeps theme headings, folded theme-phrased questions, two to three representative readings, and find-more links per theme — with wording as plain as round 2's.
- If the response narrows into a reading-list-like deliverable, the full annotation block appears (citation, source ID, study design, access level, confidence, quotes).
- No required element disappears silently; the response must not trade structure for brevity.

## Scoring Focus and Known Failure Modes

**Rubric dimensions to watch:** Structure compliance (protocol), Plain language, Intake, Workflow compliance, Task quality, Follow-up handling, Accuracy and boundaries, Efficiency.

**Known failure modes:**

- Round 2 regression persists: required elements still dropped despite rule 7 and the safeguard.
- Structure returns but clarity regresses toward round 1 stiffness (bookkeeping language, API mechanics, or internal vocabulary reappearing).
- The model interprets "deviate only when the request clearly fits no template" as license to deviate broadly.
- Turn 1 grows even longer than round 2's 16.5 minutes under added structure requirements.
