## Expected Behavior and Success Criteria

Round 1 behaviors must still hold: Turn 1 stays quick with honest quick-pass language; Turn 2 volunteers a one-line expectation statement before deep work; escalation preserves state; Turn 3 distinguishes the two deliverables in plain language.

Template safeguard (new in this round):

- Each response type (quick overview, comprehensive bibliography) matches its template's required elements; the quick pass may be leaner but must not silently drop required elements of the template it uses.
- Effort/expectation language stays plain-language, never internal vocabulary.

## Scoring Focus and Known Failure Modes

**Rubric dimensions to watch:** Structure compliance (protocol), Plain language, Intake, Efficiency, Workflow compliance, Audience and accessibility, Follow-up handling.

**Known failure modes:**

- Turn 1 over-runs despite the explicit "quick" request ahead of a next-day meeting.
- Turn 2 starts deep work without volunteering an expectation statement.
- Turn 2's bibliography compresses annotation blocks (the round 2 failure mode).
