## Key Findings

1. **The template-defaulting safeguard works in live behavior.** The session selected and followed the matching templates across all three response types, and the manifest reference log records `response-templates.md` consultation before rendering.
2. **Efficiency improved alongside structure.** Turn 1 completed in ~6.3 minutes with full structure, versus round 2's ~16.5 minutes with compressed output.
3. **Turn 3 trust support improved.** The double-check list draws on actual run state (construct mismatch in Leow/Cui, the marginally significant Li path, the anxiety-specific gap), resolving a round 2 report improvement idea.
4. **The version-of-record citation-year rule was picked up live.** The S02 session stated "Publication years reflect the journal-issue year where Crossref distinguishes online-first from issue dates" and cited Seabrook (2016) and Marino (2018) correctly; the rule was synced minutes before the run launched.
5. **Phase 3 right-sizing behaviors are validated for the first time.** S03's quick-pass scope, unprompted expectation statement, state-preserving escalation, and plain-language depth distinction all appeared in live behavior — the baseline for the PRD's phase 3 work.
